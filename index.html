A New Expression of Honey

ReHive is a creamed honey infused with real fruit, crafted in small batches with precision and care.

Each jar starts with locally sourced raw honey from a single Nottinghamshire apiary, which we churn and whip to a creamy spreadable texture, paired with vibrant fruits derived from surplus and foraged ingredients.

By pairing creamed raw honey with fruit powders made from surplus and foraged ingredients, we created something new and exciting, in the form of a dual‑colour swirled honey that’s vibrant, indulgent, and naturally flavourful. It’s honey, reimagined.

Our Story

ReHive was created by four Food Science students at the University of Nottingham, working in collaboration with Rigsby Seasonal Foods and Dr James Marsh. What began as a student brief quickly evolved into a mission to rethink how surplus fruit could be transformed into something extraordinary.

Crafted With Purpose

We work exclusively with single‑apiary Nottinghamshire honey, minimally processed and never overheated. This preserves the aroma, nutrients, and complexity of the raw honey, keeping its natural flavour and character.

Why Surplus Fruit?

Traditional freeze‑drying consumes huge amounts of energy. Cold vacuum‑drying offered a gentler, lower‑energy alternative that preserves colour, aroma, and nutrients. By using surplus fruit from supermarkets, charities, and foraging, we give new life to ingredients that would otherwise go to waste.

Small Batch. Big Flavour.

Every jar is crafted in small batches and hand‑finished with care. The result is a honey that’s indulgent, colourful, and genuinely packed with the flavour of real fruit.
